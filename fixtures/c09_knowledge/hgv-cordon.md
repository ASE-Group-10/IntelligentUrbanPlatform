# 4 Heavy goods vehicles in the city centre (demo data)

## 4.1 Purpose

The city-centre cordon keeps heavy goods vehicles out of the busiest streets during the day so that buses, cyclists and pedestrians can move safely.

## 4.2 City-centre cordon

Heavy goods vehicles with five or more axles are not permitted to enter the city-centre cordon between 07:00 and 19:00 unless a valid permit is held.
